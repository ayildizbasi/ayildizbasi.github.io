About

I am a Clinical Associate Professor of Management Science in the Department of Information Systems, Supply Chain, and Analytics at the University of Alabama in Huntsville. Before joining UAH in 2025, I taught at Worcester Polytechnic Institute's Business School, where I was also a postdoctoral fellow, and I was an Assistant Professor of Industrial Engineering at Ankara Yildirim Beyazit University in Turkey. I hold a Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from Eskisehir Osmangazi University and an M.S. in Engineering Management from Syracuse University.

My research is about supply chains and how to make them circular, safe, and resilient. I study circular and closed-loop supply chains, particularly for lithium-ion batteries and renewable energy, and how blockchain, artificial intelligence, and other digital technologies change the way supply chains are run. I work with simulation, optimization, and multi-criteria decision making, and more recently I have been examining how supply chains hold up under geopolitical and policy disruption.

My work has appeared in the International Journal of Production Research, the Journal of Cleaner Production, Resources, Conservation and Recycling, Business Strategy and the Environment, and Renewable Energy, among others. Since 2022 I have been listed among the World's Top 2% Scientists (Stanford University / Elsevier).
